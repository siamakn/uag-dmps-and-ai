What this is

Every data management plan on Zenodo that could be found by five different queries, joined to CORDIS so each one carries a discipline and a position in its project's life. The job is to pick 10: nine filling a 3 × 3 discipline × lifecycle matrix, plus one challenging case.

How the two axes are derived

  • Discipline is the top-level OECD field from the project's EuroSciVoc terms in CORDIS, not a judgement about the DMP. Records with no CORDIS match show no CORDIS match and are hidden as soon as a discipline filter is on.
  • Lifecycle stage is where the publication date falls between the project's CORDIS start and end dates: early up to 34 %, mid up to 70 %, late above. The bar in the column shows the exact position.

Reading a row

Click a title to expand the description, project dates, call id and DOI. A green maDMP tag means the record carries a real RDA DMP Common Standard JSON file; schema clean means it has no missing required field, which only 25 of 377 manage. Red tags mark sensitive-data signals — the pool for the challenging case.

Choosing well

Selection is meant to run off metadata, not off how good a plan looks. Filter down to a defensible pool, then take what falls in the cell you still need. The tray warns when two picks come from the same project, when a fourth discipline creeps in, or when a pick has no lifecycle stage.

Where your selection goes

Served locally it is written to data/selection.json on every change. Opened as a standalone file it lives in this browser only. Either way the three Copy buttons put it on the clipboard.
