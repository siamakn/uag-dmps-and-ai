That comparison has prior work. The proposed contribution is an auditable pilot
of six same-project, same-scope DMP version pairs, explicit issue-date eligibility,
a calibrated lifecycle-sensitive reference and pinned DMP-EVA configurations.
There is no established claim to be the first paired-version study. Some newly
identified close work still needs full-text inspection before manuscript novelty
language is justified.

**A matching criterion ID is not sufficient measurement equivalence.** The
semantic audit finds altered alternatives or applicability in 4a end, 5a early,
5b early and 5c end. Missing native 3c/5d remain coverage gaps, while the blank
publication mid-project 5d remains instrument undefined. The native prompt's
information exposure is a separate issue from the loaded source rubric.
Neither a shared ID nor similar 0/1/2 scale labels validate a score conversion.

**Document stage and scoring phase remain separate.** The publication explicitly
combines proposal/early, and sets no calendar-fraction boundaries for rubric
phases. Post-award status therefore does not automatically force mid-project
scoring. For calibration, initial-strategy versus final-outcome expectations is
a reasonable option to consider; implementation versus final outcomes, a common
end-state readiness lens and declared phase sensitivity have different constructs
or workload. No phase choice was made by the agents.

**Evidence limits must be visible.** An end-project criterion can concern actions
whose records are outside a standalone DMP. DMP-only review measures what the
document evidences, not proof of actual implementation, legal compliance or
achieved FAIRness. External-artifact verification needs a separate, frozen
evidence boundary and comparable access across conditions.
