## Specific implications for the proposed six-project paired pilot

These are methodological inferences/recommendations, not empirical results or newly approved protocol changes:

1. **Make the reference and its coverage the contribution.** The proposed full-packet independent ratings, retained pre-adjudication responses and explicit missing/applicability states would answer a more specific question than selected AI-exposed validation. Preserve reviewer exposure history; report all scheduled criterion rows and reasons for exclusions (contrast R1 p16 with the local experiment protocol's reference/analysis proposals).
2. **Keep retrieval and judgment failures separable.** R1 pp12–13,16,20 motivate recording missed evidence, unsupported conclusions, applicability mistakes and rubric ambiguity separately. Existing plans to retain submitted input, evidence locators, raw outputs and configuration hashes make these mechanisms inspectable. Matching full evidence exposure in F0/F1 remains necessary before attributing any difference to task decomposition.
3. **Report stability separately from agreement.** R1 p17 directly illustrates their divergence. Six projects remain six clusters; twelve versions and many criteria cannot manufacture a large independent N. Provide case-level results, denominators and repeat variation where actually measured, rather than importing either prior study's headline rate as a target or expected effect.
4. **Position the paired provenance narrowly.** A defensible prospective contribution is a small open-access European, six-discipline, same-scope paired-document pilot with issue-date eligibility, provenance, explicit lifecycle expectations and a pinned DMP-EVA comparison. R1 does not report this design; T1's methods are uninspected. This supports a concrete contribution description, **not** a claim to be first, a discipline-wide conclusion, a causal lifecycle effect or an exhaustive literature gap.

The local human pilot remains deferred. The immediate remaining literature dependency is access to T1's identified methods/evaluation pages; no reference labels, experiment freeze or benchmark execution follows from this report.
