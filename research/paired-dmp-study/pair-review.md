# Independent review of proposed DMP pairs

Sources checked 2026-11-03; completed AGRICORE review persisted 2026-11-04 without new research. **Six distinct proposed discipline slots have supported candidates: iMagine, EGI-ACE, SUSTRACK, RECHARGE, NANOFACTS and AGRICORE.** AGRICORE replaces RECAP for agriculture; RECAP's timing and lineage pass, but current agricultural membership is unsupported. This is a supported proposal with the date and rights caveats below, not a frozen selection or human reference assessment of DMP quality. No application or model was run, no documents were scored, and no production data or selection was changed.

Applied decisions: D08 permits any documented CORDIS branch membership, one slot per project, with the local dominant branch reported; D09 uses the latest authoritative project end, including extensions. The [JSON companion](pair-review.json) preserves source labels, exact dates/day counts, file hashes, source provenance and bounded uncertainties.

## Medical and agricultural decisions

**NANOFACTS is eligible as a candidate for the medical slot under D08/D09, with recorded early-date uncertainty.** Fresh CORDIS explicitly includes oncology and pharmacology under medical and health sciences, as well as engineering classifications. It gives 2021-01-01–2024-06-30. The early cover says 36 months and the late cover 42 months, corroborating a six-month duration change. The original end of 2023-12-31 is inferred from the start plus the earlier stated duration; no amendment register was independently inspected. A sensitivity calculation against that inferred original end gives early 169–180/1,094 = 15.45–16.45% and late 1,367/1,094 = 124.95%. Qualification is unchanged, while the current extended end remains the primary basis.

**AGRICORE supports the agricultural slot under D08/D09.** The inspected current CORDIS page lists one natural path (biological sciences → ecology → ecosystems) and one agricultural path (agriculture, forestry, and fisheries → agriculture). There is no inherited dominant-branch value because the project is absent from the local dataset; the current path counts tie. D08 permits its agricultural assignment, with that tie reported.

Both PDFs print the original project interval 2019-09-01–2023-08-31 (48 months). Current CORDIS instead ends the project on 2024-06-30. That extended authoritative end governs. The selected version dates 2020-02-28 and 2024-08-31 satisfy both windows against the current end. The early Ares receipt 2020-02-29 is retained separately: the two statements span 180–181/1,764 days (10.20–10.26%), but the receipt is not substituted for the version-history date. The late source string `31-ago-2024` is the final v1.0 revision date, normalized to 2024-08-31. Against the explicitly printed original end, the denominator is 1,460 days; early is 12.33% and late 125.07%, so the extension does not change qualification. The late PDF retains the old project interval; the current CORDIS value is not silently replaced by that stale field. No complete amendment register was inspected.

Exact sources are [D9.2](https://agricore-project.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/D9.2-1.pdf), SHA-256 `83a0aaa24f920828fc564ad3154d0c87ce8015ea5126426f53cd6d3e466e5f88` (698,672 bytes), and [D9.3](https://agricore-project.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/D9.3-Final-Data-Management-Plan.pdf), SHA-256 `b4081e3a26704a53d9ce00f99586e0284f92fbc8a2eabc5887de71e7c8d663aa` (807,671 bytes). Both history pages were visually checked. Unknown blanket reuse terms do not add an unapproved exclusion criterion; a source-links-and-hashes manifest can preserve this limitation without asserting redistribution or model-upload permission.

**RECAP has an adequately evidenced post-end final issue, but cannot currently fill the agricultural slot.** Late p2's explicit “Issue Date” for final version 3.0 is 2018-12-03, agreeing with its separate “Date of Delivery” field. “Actual” delivery on that page instead says 2018-10-31. The selected final revision has its own later issue date; treating the old delivery statement as an interchangeable issue date would erase this version distinction. Preserve the inconsistency, but it does not negate the specifically dated final revision. Early actual delivery 2017-04-30 is also distinct from its final issue 2017-04-25, and both are early.

The **RECAP membership gate is substantive**: fresh CORDIS at 2026-11-03T17:42:09Z lists social sciences, engineering and technology, and natural sciences. Its Fields of Science section has no agricultural-sciences branch. The saved dataset lists agriculture, but a historical raw CORDIS source establishing that membership was not retained. Neither agricultural subject matter nor a cached search reader's contrary summary establishes the required branch against the inspected source. RECAP remains a timing/lineage-checked alternative for its evidenced memberships and is excluded from the proposed agricultural assignment; AGRICORE fills that slot instead.

## Verification and limits

PDF skill used for read-only inspection. The two date/version pages for EGI-ACE and SUSTRACK, the early and late iMagine description pages, RECHARGE's early header/revision and late cover/update pages, NANOFACTS' early cover/legal-notice footer and late issue/history/scope pages, both AGRICORE history pages, and both RECAP document-history pages were visually checked against extracted text. Lineage passages were checked in full extracted text. All reported day counts were independently recalculated with calendar-day subtraction and integer eligibility comparisons.

Current CORDIS endpoints and relevant field paths confirm all six proposed slot memberships and expose the RECAP discrepancy. Available dominant branches come from the local dataset; CORDIS does not label those branches “dominant,” and AGRICORE's tie is reported explicitly. Matching current end dates to earlier stated project duration supports consistency, but does **not** prove that no extension or historical amendment ever existed. The latest end at this snapshot governs the calculation; no complete amendment register was inspected. A later authoritative correction must trigger recalculation, particularly for iMagine.

The evidence supports a six-project, twelve-document proposal with the limits above; RECAP remains an excluded agricultural reserve. No human-reference validation, exhaustive repository search or frozen cohort follows. Recency remains a preference: RECAP's age is not the reason its agricultural assignment fails. This review does not independently settle FAIR-EASE's express reuse restriction or authorize publication of third-party documents or model submission.
