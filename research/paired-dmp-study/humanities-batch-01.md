## Coverage and limitations

Six full Zenodo record API responses were retrieved, along with four PDFs (the two E-RIHS and two DIAMAS documents). Record-level metadata included concept IDs and version API links. Each of six version-list requests returned HTTP 403; the three grant-specific Zenodo search requests also returned 403, so version totals and search pagination are **unknown**. These failures prevent any claim that the observed records are the latest or exhaustive. CORDIS fact sheets and results pages were retrieved for all three. The DIAMAS results page lists three DMP deliverables, including Update 2; its linked EC download produced an HTML page rather than the requested PDF, so no issue date was inferred from a delivery deadline, link or upload. The project's [work-package page](https://diamasproject.eu/objectives/managing-and-coordinating-the-project/) lists D1.6 as an update but a deadline is not an issue date. The project's [results page](https://diamasproject.eu/the-results/) listed D1.1 when inspected; it does not establish absence of other versions.

The four PDFs were read with text extraction and relevant version-history pages visually rendered. Scope judgments describe document identity, not DMP quality. Next useful bounded step is to recover the DIAMAS Update 2 from its source or another authoritative repository, verify its actual version issue date and scope, then seek a genuinely post-end version. E-RIHS needs a later document in the *same* chosen scope; HeriTACE cannot currently satisfy the strict post-end rule with the authoritative future end date.
