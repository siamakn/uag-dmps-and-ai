## Approved calibration scale and draft anchors

Use named scale **`ordinal_012_calibration_v1`**, approved for calibration: integer **0**, **1** or **2**, with null for non-scored dispositions. Proposed anchors to test are **0** = applicable requirement not evidenced after checking the relevant accessible packet; **1** = partially evidenced, with specified missing elements; **2** = evidenced to the stated criterion/phase anchor. The user approved testing this scale; detailed criterion anchors and their phase interpretation still need calibration. A complete score does not establish that practices were actually performed or legally compliant.

The August meeting's 1–10/mean suggestion is historical; it does not block or replace the approved 0/1/2 calibration. Freeze final benchmark anchors and aggregation after calibration, preserving original values and scale versions. Do not convert to a 0–100 “gold standard.” Treat the scores as ordinal unless a separately justified analysis is approved.

Keep independent dimensions: applicability (`applicable`, `not_applicable`, `uncertain`), evidence state (`evidenced`, `partly_evidenced`, `not_reported`, `unreadable`, `unavailable_in_packet`, `uncertain`, `instrument_undefined`), score or null, confidence and rationale. NA requires a criterion-specific reason and supporting source or documented contextual judgment. Silence is not NA. Unreadable/truncated material is not evidence of absence.

For this named scale, an entered **2** requires applicable + evidenced; **1** requires applicable + partly evidenced; **0** requires applicable + not reported, explicit confirmation that the relevant accessible packet was searched, and a search-scope note. Evidence for a separate aspect does not prove satisfaction of the criterion. NA, uncertain applicability, uncertain evidence, unreadable/unavailable material, and instrument-undefined entries require null scores. A blank score is `not_entered`, never zero; `withheld` records a deliberate inability to score. Confidence cannot substitute for missing evidence. The form must preserve the raw human response and ask for clarification of inconsistent combinations rather than silently invent, coerce or discard labels.

This reviewer-independence rule does **not** prohibit separately authorized technical model smoke tests before labels exist. Clearly label such runs as technical/diagnostic, retain their settings/exposure history, and make no accuracy or AI-human agreement claim from them. A single tool evidence unit mapped to several IDs does not become independent observations.
